Plan-to-BIM Takeoff

A floor plan image is segmented by a CV model, turned into walls, doors, windows and rooms, calibrated to metres from door widths, and built into a 3D model. The system then measures quantities and prices them, and shows how the cost could move depending on the month construction starts.

From image to geometry the real CV output behind this model

When to build material part of the cost follows forecast price indices

Each cost item maps to BLS producer price indices (e.g. partitions: 40% softwood lumber, 60% gypsum). Half of each item's cost is treated as material. Labour is held at today's rate. The line is an ARIMA forecast. The band is the 10th–90th percentile of ARIMA's own past forecast errors, replayed jointly across materials from every historical start point since 2016.
